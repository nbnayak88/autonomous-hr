# HR Capability Model

The Autonomous HR capability model organizes HR around business capabilities rather than software modules.

## Capability Domains

- Workforce Foundations
- Talent Acquisition
- Performance and Organization
- Growth and Leadership
- Rewards and Performance
- Learning and Mobility
- Payroll
- Time and Operations
- Workforce Intelligence
- Digital Employee Experience
- AI-Powered HR
- Connected HR

Each capability domain maps to one of the 12 HR architecture streams in the master repository.
