# BAISI PAHACHA — HR Tech Interview Framework

| Phase | # | Theme | Candidate must demonstrate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| KNOW | 01 | Domain Foundation | HR domain, actors, terminology and outcomes |
| KNOW | 02 | Product / Technology Knowledge | Relevant HR technology capabilities |
| KNOW | 03 | Process & Business Context | Technology connected to HR process and business need |
| KNOW | 04 | Data & Information Model | HR objects, relationships, lifecycle and data quality |
| DESIGN | 05 | Requirement Analysis | Requirements, constraints and acceptance criteria |
| DESIGN | 06 | Solution Design | Fit-for-purpose HR solution and rationale |
| DESIGN | 07 | Configuration / Development | Configuration, extension and development decisions |
| DESIGN | 08 | Integration & Architecture | Connected HR ecosystem and integration patterns |
| DELIVER | 09 | Testing & Quality Assurance | Functional, integration, security and experience quality |
| DELIVER | 10 | Deployment & Release | Release, cutover, rollback and adoption |
| DELIVER | 11 | Migration & Cutover | Data integrity and business continuity |
| DELIVER | 12 | Operations & Support | Monitoring, governance and support |
| SOLVE | 13 | Troubleshooting & Root Cause Analysis | Systematic diagnosis |
| SOLVE | 14 | Scenario-Based Problem Solving | Decisions under realistic constraints |
| SOLVE | 15 | Risk, Controls & Security | Employee-data protection, access and compliance |
| SOLVE | 16 | Performance & Optimization | Reliability, scalability, usability and value |
| INFLUENCE | 17 | Stakeholder Management | HR, IT, business and implementation alignment |
| INFLUENCE | 18 | Communication & Consulting | Complex architecture explained in business language |
| INFLUENCE | 19 | Presales / Leadership / Decision Making | Options, recommendations and executive decisions |
| TRANSFORM | 20 | Transformation & Roadmap | Current-state to future-state transformation |
| TRANSFORM | 21 | Innovation & Emerging Technology | AI, agents, automation and emerging HR technology |
| TRANSFORM | 22 | Enterprise Architecture & Business Value | Enterprise coherence and measurable business value |

## STAR+ Format

S — Situation
T — Task
A — Action
R — Result
SME Probe — follow-up that exposes depth
Reflection — learning and next-architecture decision

## Candidate Answer Spine

Business Context → HR Problem → Process Diagnosis → Technology Decision → Architecture → Implementation → Integration → Risk & Security → Testing → Adoption → Business Result → Transformation Opportunity
