### 1. Discover

### Purpose
The purpose of the Discover phase is for the customer to understand the breadth, depth, and functionality of the SAP SuccessFactors solution.

During this phase, the customer and implementation team discuss:
- Overall HR strategy
- Key business drivers and priorities
- Key success metrics
- Target solution scope and HR business processes
- High‑level functionality requirements
- Initial implementation scenarios
By the end of Discover, the customer and sales team reach a jointly agreed description of:
- Implementation scope
- Deployment approach and roadmap
- Overall project timelines
- Target solution model

## 🔬 3. Explore

### 🔬 3. Explore

### 🔬 3. Explore

### Purpose
The purpose of the Explore phase is to perform a **fit‑to‑standard analysis** to:
- Validate that the solution functionality in scope meets the business requirements
- Confirm that requirements can be satisfied with standard processes and configuration
- Identify and document gaps and required configuration values
Identified gaps and configuration items are added to the backlog for use in the Realize phase.

### Explore Activities
- Prepare, set up, and conduct solution validation workshops
- Refine and clarify business requirements based on workshop outcomes
- Identify master data and organizational requirements
- Confirm the to‑be business processes (process hierarchy and variants)
- Define the functional solution design, including gap analysis, in solution design workshops
- Associate business requirements to process steps and solution components
- Obtain business sign‑off on delta requirements and design documents
- Collect end‑user information, analyze learning needs, and develop a learning deployment strategy
- Establish project management, tracking, and reporting for value delivery during Explore
### How Explore Is Delivered
In the Explore phase, industry and solution experts from SAP (or the implementation partner) lead a series of structured “show‑and‑tell” and solution design workshops. The goals are to:
- Review best‑practice functionality of the SAP SuccessFactors solution
- Identify delta requirements or gaps
- Document the conceptual design of the to‑be SAP SuccessFactors solution

## 🏗️ 4. Realize

### 🏗️ 4. Realize

### Purpose
The purpose of the Realize phase is to **incrementally build and test** an integrated business and system environment based on:
- The process requirements identified in Explore
- The backlog of configuration, extensions, and integrations
During Realize, the project team configures the solution, loads customer data, plans adoption activities, and prepares cutover and operations for go‑live.

### Realize Phase Activities
- Monitor project activities and manage scope, risks, and issues
- Configure the solution in the Development system using incremental builds and time‑boxed iterations
- Replicate the configured solution in the Test environment
- Migrate data in preparation for testing cycles
- Conduct overall end‑to‑end testing of business processes in the Test environment
- Conduct project team and key‑user training on the configured solution
- Set up the production environment and verify technical readiness
- Finalize end‑user training materials and documentation
- Plan detailed cutover activities and rollback strategies
- Track and report on value delivery against agreed objectives
### How Realize Is Delivered
The project team uses a series of iterations to:
- Configure, test, confirm, and document the end‑to‑end solution
- Load and validate data
- Work closely with business representatives to ensure good fit of the built solution
- Release results of multiple iterations early to business users to accelerate time‑to‑value and provide early access to finalized functionalities

### 🚀 5. Deploy

- Conduct final system tests

- Identify and document all issues encountered during transition to the new solution

- Production support processes
